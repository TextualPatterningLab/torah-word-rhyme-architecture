Demonstrative distribution of word-final correspondences under the documented final-marked-vowel heuristic. Matching: STRICT normalized segment identity; identical normalized words: include. Each eligible word is compared with the preceding 20 eligible words. Displayed metric: Local proportion of eligible words participating in ≥1 accepted correspondence. Local windows contain up to 250 eligible words and advance by 50 eligible words. Horizontal position is normalized separately to 0–100% of each book's eligible-word sequence; equal strip widths do not represent equal book lengths. The n labels give eligible-word counts. Matching distances and local windows use actual word counts, not percentages. The shared color scale represents local proportions, not absolute numbers of words. The map illustrates the operational rule; it is not a test of poetic status or a complete model of Hebrew lexical stress.
